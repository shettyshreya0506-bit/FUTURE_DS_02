# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Internship (Task 2)

### Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer retention and churn patterns in a subscription-based telecommunications business using Power BI.

The objective is to identify why customers leave, understand customer behavior, analyze retention trends, and provide actionable recommendations to reduce customer churn.

### Objectives
- Analyze customer churn and retention rates.
- Identify major reasons for customer churn.
- Understand churn patterns across different contract types.
- Analyze customer lifetime using tenure.
- Compare customer behavior across internet and payment methods.
- Provide recommendations to improve customer retention.

### Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- CSV Dataset

### Dataset
**Dataset:** Telecom Customer Churn

The dataset contains customer information such as:
- Customer ID
- Age and gender
- Contract type
- Internet service
- Monthly charges
- Customer status
- Churn category
- Churn reason
- Tenure in months
- Payment method

### Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes:

1. Total Customers
2. Churned Customers
3. Stayed Customers
4. Customer Retention Rate
5. Customer Churn Rate
6. Customer Churn by Contract Type
7. Customer Churn by Reason Category
8. Revenue by Customer Status
9. Customer Churn by Tenure Group
10. Average Customer Lifetime by Status
11. Customer Churn by Internet Service
12. Customer Churn by Payment Method
13. Customer Retention Rate by Contract Type

### Key Findings

- Total customers analyzed: 7,043.
- Customer churn rate: 28.37%.
- Customer retention rate: 71.63%.
- Month-to-month contract customers show considerably higher churn.
- Competitor-related issues are the leading reported churn category.
- Bank withdrawal accounts for the largest number of churned customers by payment method.
- Fiber Optic customers contribute significantly to customer churn.
- Customers who stayed have a higher average tenure than customers who churned.

### Business Recommendations

1. Encourage customers to choose long-term contracts through discounts and loyalty benefits.
2. Improve competitive pricing and service offerings to address competitor-related churn.
3. Investigate payment-related complaints and improve payment experiences.
4. Improve Fiber Optic service reliability and customer support.
5. Introduce early engagement programs to identify customers at risk of leaving.
6. Collect regular customer feedback and provide personalized retention offers.

### Project Deliverable
An interactive Power BI dashboard presenting customer churn patterns, retention metrics, customer lifetime analysis, and business recommendations.

### Conclusion
This analysis provides insights into customer behavior and identifies important areas where the business can improve customer satisfaction and retention. The findings can help businesses make data-driven decisions to reduce customer loss and improve long-term customer relationships.
